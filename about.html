Our mission

Legal help that respects you first

Founded in 2011 by senior advocate Daniel Okafor, JustiAid began with a single phone line and a simple belief: nobody should face the legal system confused or alone. Today we connect clients with a vetted network of 320+ advocates across every major practice area — but that belief hasn't changed.

Every advocate in our directory has verified credentials. We explain options in plain language, we never push retainers, and we'll happily recommend a free legal-aid path if that's what you need.
