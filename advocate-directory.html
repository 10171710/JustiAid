FAQ

How the advocate directory works

Getting matched with the right advocate, answered simply.

Every advocate on this directory passes an ID, bar-enrolment and practice-record check before they're listed. Those checks are re-run yearly.

Use the "Book consultation" button on any advocate card to submit a case enquiry. A specialist confirms your appointment within 48 hours.

Hit "Request a match" below the directory — we'll find a suitable specialist within your area and fee range, usually within 3 working days.

Reviews are only accepted from clients with a completed engagement, and automated screening filters out suspicious or duplicate entries.
