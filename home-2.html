Trust & safety

How every advocate gets verified

Being listed in our directory is not automatic. Each profile passes a multi-step vetting process before it ever appears — and stays under review after.

1 · Identity & bar check

We verify the advocate's government ID and confirm their bar registration and roll number directly with the state bar council where possible.

2 · Experience & specialisation

Years in practice, reported cases, specialisations and tribunal experience are checked against the advocate's bar record and references.

3 · Client reference calls

We speak to past clients about communication, transparency and outcomes before an advocate gets the verified badge.

4 · Fee transparency review

Consultation charges, retainer ranges and billing rules must be published in a clear, written format clients can read before engaging.

5 · Ongoing performance review

Every profile is re-reviewed quarterly. Ratings shift with fresh client feedback, and under-performers are removed.

Verified badge on every profile

Only advocates who clear all five steps carry the JustiAid verified badge.

Comparison guide

What to compare before you choose

Use these five factors to read any advocate's profile like a pro — and shortlist with confidence.

Factor What to look for
Specialisation Prefer advocates who handle your practice area regularly — a family-law specialist over a general practitioner.
Experience Check years in practice and the number of similar matters resolved. Relevant experience beats raw seniority.
Client reviews Read recent reviews for communication and timelines — a modest score with consistent feedback is a good sign.
Fee structure Clear consultation price and retainer range matter. Ask what's included before signing anything.
Availability Confirm court availability, response time and whether phone/video consultations are offered for your matter.
